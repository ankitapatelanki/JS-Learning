 1. What is a Promise in JavaScript? *
Promise is an object that tells whether our asynchronous operation is complete or not.
  2. What are the 3 states of a Promise? *
There are 3 states of a promises --
1. Resolve state
2.Reject state
3.Pending state
  3. How do you create a Promise? *
new promise (resolve , reject){
}

4. How do you handle the result of a Promise? *
 We can handle the result of a promise in two ways:----
 1. Resolved - the promise is successfull ,
or
2. Rejected - the promise fails
5. What is .then() in Promises? *
.then() runs when the promise is successful.
6. What is .catch() in Promises? *
.catch() runs when the promise is fail.
